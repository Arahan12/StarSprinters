Train with purpose

Warm-ups & training

Use these sessions to build safe movement, ball control, decision-making and teamwork. Adapt intensity to your group and available space.

Warm-up · 5 minutes

Dynamic movement circuit

Suitable before basketball, netball, soccer or most court/field activities.

Space: half courtEquipment: cones
  1. Jog easily around the marked area for 60 seconds.
  2. Complete high knees, heel flicks and side shuffles between cones.
  3. Add controlled lunges and arm circles.
  4. Finish with 3 short accelerations, slowing down safely.
Basketball · 8 minutes

Dribble control

Develop confidence with both hands and keep your eyes up.

Equipment: basketballsPairs or solo
  1. Stationary low dribbles with the dominant hand, then the other.
  2. Move through cones while keeping the ball close.
  3. Change direction at each cone without pushing into others.
  4. Partner mirrors your movement at walking pace.
Basketball · 8 minutes

Passing in motion

Build accurate passing, timing and communication.

Equipment: ballsGroups of 3
  1. Form a triangle with safe spacing.
  2. Chest-pass and step toward the target.
  3. Follow the pass to the next cone or position.
  4. Call the receiver's name and show hands early.
Basketball · 10 minutes

Layup progression

Practise footwork and controlled finishing from both sides.

Equipment: hoop, ballSmall groups
  1. Rehearse the two-step pattern without a ball.
  2. Walk through a layup from one side.
  3. Add a gentle dribble approach when ready.
  4. Swap sides and give constructive feedback.
Team tactics · 10 minutes

3-on-2 decision game

Practise spacing, quick passing and defensive recovery.

Equipment: ball, conesHalf court
  1. Three attackers start against two defenders.
  2. Attackers must make a pass before attempting a shot.
  3. Defenders communicate and protect the basket without unsafe contact.
  4. Rotate roles after each possession.
Cool-down · 4 minutes

Reset & reflect

Help the team finish calmly and set a goal for next time.

No equipmentWhole team
  1. Walk until breathing begins to settle.
  2. Complete gentle, pain-free mobility movements.
  3. Each player shares one positive team moment.
  4. Agree on one skill to improve next session.

Sample 35-minute training session

0–5 min · Dynamic warm-up5–12 min · Dribbling12–20 min · Passing & movement20–30 min · Small-sided game30–35 min · Cool-down & review

Safety: check the playing area and equipment, use suitable footwear, allow water breaks, and stop if someone is injured or unwell.

Know the game

Basketball guide

A quick reference for the sport Star Sprinters played. This section covers the history and main rules of basketball, the sport featured in the supplied records.

01 / HISTORY

Where basketball began

Basketball was invented by Dr James Naismith in 1891 in Springfield, Massachusetts, USA. He designed an indoor team game to keep students active during winter. The first game used a soccer ball and peach baskets as goals.

The game developed as rules, equipment and playing styles changed. The baskets were eventually replaced by open hoops, dribbling evolved, and organised leagues helped basketball grow into an international professional sport, including the NBA in the United States and FIBA-governed international competition.

Historical overview supplied as general background; the supplied team documents do not contain a basketball history section.

02 / MAIN RULES

Rules to remember

  1. Score: a free throw is 1 point, a field goal inside the three-point line is 2, and a shot beyond it is 3.
  2. Dribbling: bounce the ball while moving; carrying or restarting a dribble after stopping is a violation.
  3. Travelling: do not take illegal steps while holding the ball.
  4. Contact: avoid pushing, holding or illegal body contact; fouls may lead to free throws or possession.
  5. Out of bounds: the ball is out when it touches a boundary line or something outside the court.
  6. Team play: follow the competition's timing, possession and substitution rules, which may vary by format.
03 / OFFICIATING

Fair play for scorers & umpires

Apply the same rules to both teams, record scores accurately, communicate decisions calmly and avoid favouritism. If unsure, pause and check the agreed competition rules.

04 / GAME PLAN

Star Sprinters focus points

Protect possession, communicate before passing, take balanced shots, finish layups under control, recover on defence and keep morale high after mistakes.
